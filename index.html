Material presets are typical carbide end-mill starting points for a 1/4"–3/8" tool. Scale chip load up for larger cutters and down for smaller or less rigid setups. Round-over and other profile bits engage the material at a changing depth through the cut, so treat that mode's numbers as a cautious starting point and dial feed down further on figured or veneered faces. Plywood's glue lines are abrasive and prone to splintering on the exit face — a sharp bit and a sacrificial backer help more than any speed adjustment. Always verify against your tool manufacturer's data.
